**Record And Play**

Record And Play will add a record button to record the audio and play button to listen it. Please browse record and play from activity browser and add to the stage.


***Pro tip***

Place the record-play button where ever you want to place in the stage and resize it.

***Note***

Use only one record and play in a single stage.

In preview record button icon will be 60% of its actual dimension.
